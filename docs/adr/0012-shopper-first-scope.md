# ADR 0012 — Restore the complete shopper outing as the product scope

- Decision date: 2026-10-06 (America/Chicago).
- Status: Product Owner approved; documentation adoption does not activate runtime capabilities.
- Authorization: “I approve this cleanup and scope,” following the proposed product boundary and recommendations for private unlisted stops, suggested order with manual adjustment, and persistent capacity-based photos with provisional prices.
- Supersedes: ADR0009's store-only product priority; current-trip/private-memory deferral; mandatory full synthetic Owner business suite; conflicting monthly-upload/deletion/commercial descriptions. Does not supersede existing deployment, data, provider or security obligations.

## Context

The original gap was finding stores across websites/social media, sharing them between spouses, planning an outing around location/hours, and retaining private memories. Directory-only progress did not complete that job. Team, collaboration, offline, billing and release programs accumulated alongside disconnected core actions.

## Decision

[PRD](../../PRD.md) owns the complete discover → favorite/share → Add to Trip → plan → visit → remember scope. Simple owner-managed listings support the outing and optional photo capacity funds the ad-free service. One organizer and one responsible owner per store are the first target. Keep the existing stack and approved visual/accessibility system; preserve useful code.

Shared public store links are core. Private shared-trip views, simultaneous editing, recipient inboxes and mutable offline synchronization are deferred. Photos persist within capacity rather than being deleted each month. Exact paid prices/capacities are unresolved; no paid launch is a prerequisite for the unpaid learning milestone.

## Consequences and preservation

Reconcile current specs and backlog before new implementation leaves. The approval is for documentation/scope cleanup, not bulk feature implementation, data deletion, provider changes, deployment, contact or spending. Preserve source, existing grants, private data, consent, media rights and any subscriber obligations. Do not fabricate old gate receipts to unlock simpler behavior.

ADR0010 and ADR0011 retain their exact existing exposure and real-store boundaries. Features being in target scope does not enable them in the current public test. For future admitted trips, applicable routing/location/privacy controls apply; old trips/regional/cohort/team completeness is not a prerequisite to unrelated selected work.

## Acceptance

Current documents agree on core/deferred behavior, commercial uncertainty and existing-exposure boundaries. A dated implementation/backlog map identifies retained work and gaps without treating code presence as proof. Future product acceptance is the connected real outing and owner evaluation in PRD, not ticket count or a synthetic business-management demonstration.


## Approved audit reconciliation — 2026-10-06

Product Owner: “lets take your rec.” Apply the focused documentation recommendations: retire future mandatory media migration/unlimited-storage assumptions, archive stale planning contracts, distinguish history from current exposure, and clarify memories and destinations within this scope. Retained-data/security/recovery duties stay intact. The existing capability specification owns those detailed behaviors; no new collection, collaboration, AI or commercial feature is approved.
