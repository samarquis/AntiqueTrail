# QR Partner Invitations Start Onboarding, Not Authorization

An Administrator with MFA and recent authentication may generate an in-person Store Partner Invitation after a Synthetic Store demonstration and verbal interest. The QR contains only a high-entropy opaque token whose hash is stored server-side; it embeds no owner, store, email, or role data, expires after 30 minutes or one successful consumption, and may be revoked. Scanning opens the existing PWA onboarding flow but grants nothing.

The owner first reviews the pilot terms and enters acknowledgements, typed name/title/store, and normalized owner-controlled email. Password and MFA credentials never enter application tables. One idempotent application-database transaction consumes the invitation, stores immutable provisional consent, and creates one application-only unprivileged Pending Partner Identity keyed by a purpose-specific email HMAC. Failure rolls back all three; a committed retry resumes the same record and never unconsumes the invitation.

Supabase Auth signup/sign-in is a separate system boundary. `bind_partner_identity` derives the current verified Auth email/user from the session, requires MFA, locks and matches the pending HMAC record, binds one Auth user once, and finalizes the Pilot Consent Receipt in one application transaction. An existing Auth account signs in normally. Interrupted signup leaves the pending record resumable and unprivileged; an orphan Auth account remains an ordinary shopper. Unbound pending records expire after 30 days and delete typed PII/provisional consent within 24 hours, leaving only a content-free invitation result. Only final binding allows a Pilot Store Draft. The pending identity still has no store, role, scope, or pilot-data grant.

Published-business-contact authority verification and separate Administrator approval remain required. Final Pilot Store Draft approval atomically creates the Pilot Store Record and exact store-scoped Store Representative role. After approval, status-only email links to normal PWA sign-in and never carries a reusable invitation, role, or authorization token; verified-email/MFA authentication and the active store grant control portal access. This preserves the in-person QR handoff while preventing a photographed, stolen, or forwarded link from becoming an authorization credential. Generation, expiry, revocation, consumption, provisional submission, identity creation, email/MFA verification, receipt finalization, authority review, final approval, grant, and activation handoff are audited.
