# QR Partner Invitations Start Onboarding, Not Authorization

An Administrator with MFA and recent authentication may generate an in-person Store Partner Invitation after a Synthetic Store demonstration and verbal interest. The QR contains only a high-entropy opaque token whose hash is stored server-side; it embeds no owner, store, email, or role data, expires after 30 minutes or one successful redemption, and may be revoked. Scanning opens the existing PWA onboarding flow but grants nothing. Store Partner Pilot Consent, verified owner-controlled email, MFA, published-business-contact authority verification, and separate Administrator approval are required before the Pilot Store Record and store-scoped Store Representative role are created. After approval, status-only email links to normal PWA sign-in and never carries a reusable invitation, role, or authorization token; verified-email/MFA authentication and the active store grant control portal access. This preserves the convenience of an in-person QR handoff while preventing a photographed, stolen, or forwarded link from becoming an authorization credential; every transition and activation handoff is audited.
